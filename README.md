# Company Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, and regional sales distribution. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and helps identify trends and patterns to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify top-performing countries by revenue.
- Understand revenue contribution across sales channels.
- Compare revenue distribution across European regions.
- Enable interactive analysis using slicers.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel**
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- KPI Cards and Dashboard Formatting

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks the following metrics:
- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

## 📉 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Profit
A line chart comparing monthly revenue and gross profit to identify trends and changes in business performance.

### 2. Top 5 Country by Revenue
A horizontal bar chart highlighting the leading countries by revenue.

### 3. Revenue Share by Channel
A doughnut or pie chart showing the contribution of different sales channels, including Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, and Retail Partners.

### 4. Region-wise Comparison
A doughnut chart comparing the revenue distribution across Central Europe, Northern Europe, Southern Europe, and Western Europe.

### 5. Country-wise Revenue
A horizontal bar chart comparing revenue across countries to identify differences in market performance.

## 🔍 Interactive Features
- Product Category slicer
- Country slicer
- Quarter slicer
- Interactive PivotCharts and KPI analysis

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different categories, countries, and quarters.

## 💡 Business Value
This dashboard helps users:
- Track revenue and profitability at a glance.
- Identify high-performing countries and sales channels.
- Evaluate monthly performance trends.
- Compare regional revenue contribution.
- Explore business performance using interactive filters.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download `Excel_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx` from this repository.
2. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the **Dashboard** sheet.
4. Use the available slicers to filter the data.
5. Explore the KPIs and charts to understand sales performance.

**Note:** For the best experience with PivotCharts and slicers, use the desktop version of Microsoft Excel.
